Marc Márquez is a legendary Spanish Grand Prix motorcycle racer and nine-time World Champion, widely regarded as one of the greatest and most aggressive riders in motorsport history. Born on February 17, 1993, in Cervera, Spain, he made his world championship debut in 2008. He rapidly climbed the ranks to become the youngest-ever MotoGP premier-class champion as a rookie in 2013 at just 20 years old. Riding for the Repsol Honda Team, his spectacular, physics-defying cornering style and extreme lean angles allowed him to dominate the 2010s, capturing six premier-class titles between 2013 and 2019. Following a severe, career-threatening arm injury in 2020 that required four major surgeries, Márquez showcased incredible resilience. He departed Honda to revive his career, completing one of sports history's greatest comebacks by capturing his seventh MotoGP world title in 2025 after joining the Ducati Lenovo Team.
